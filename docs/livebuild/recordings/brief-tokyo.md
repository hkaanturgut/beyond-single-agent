# Tokyo — 3 days on $120.00 (total)
One-line summary: 3-day Tokyo trip for a single traveler on a tight $120.00 total — basic dorm/capsule nights, convenience-store meals, Suica for cheap metro trips, and a focus on free/low-cost highlights (Asakusa, Meiji Jingu, Shibuya, Ueno/Chidorigafuchi for hanami).

Day-by-day outline
- Day 1 — Arrival, settle, Asakusa & Senso‑ji
  - Arrive, buy/load Suica (Suica deposit $5.00 included in budget), check in to budget dorm/capsule (book outside central wards to hit $20.00/night).
  - Walk Asakusa, visit Senso‑ji (free), explore Nakamise shopping street (snacks from convenience stores or 100‑yen shop).
  - Evening: low-key stroll along Sumida River; cheap dinner from convenience store (~$10/day budget).

- Day 2 — Meiji Jingu, Harajuku & Shibuya
  - Morning: Meiji Jingu shrine and Yoyogi Park (free) — good for a peaceful walk under early-spring greenery.
  - Afternoon: Explore Harajuku streets, budget shopping/snacks (100‑yen shops), then head to Shibuya for the scramble crossing and people‑watching.
  - Evening: Night views in Shibuya; convenience-store dinner/snacks.

- Day 3 — Ueno Park / Chidorigafuchi hanami spots & departure
  - Morning: Ueno Park for cherry‑blossom viewing (seasonal) and free museum gardens (museum admission not budgeted except the $3.00 activities allowance).
  - Optional stroll at Chidorigafuchi for sakura if time allows (free).
  - Return to dorm, check out, cheapest airport transfer roundtrip component was budgeted ($10.00 overall — use cheapest option booked in advance), depart.

Practical notes and packing
- Weather (April): mild spring — pack a light jacket and umbrella.
- Meals: plan on convenience-store meals and 100‑yen shop snacks to stay within $10.00/day food allocation.
- Mobility: budget assumes metro $4.00/day × 3 and Suica $5.00 deposit; plan walking-heavy days to save fares.
- Caveats: This plan is very tight and realistic only if you accept basic dorm beds, minimal paid attractions, and convenience-store meals. There's little or no buffer for luggage storage, unexpected fares, or pricier hostels/attractions. Assumptions: international flights excluded.
